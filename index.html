Foundations of AI Literacy

Understanding and Using Generative Artificial Intelligence Responsibly

Xander Basson First Edition · 2026 DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21954982

Abstract

Generative Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming embedded in professional and personal contexts, increasing the need for practical AI literacy. This foundational literacy guide considers the capabilities and limitations of generative AI; identifies key risks associated with its use; and addresses effective prompting and the avoidance of AI stylistic tendencies in favour of authentic writing. Emphasis is placed on appropriate human oversight, with AI augmenting and not replacing critical thinking.
